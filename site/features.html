Everything SmartSpec does.

A tour of the app, one feature at a time. Scroll to see each one at work, in the app itself.

Focus

A board you arrange

What you’re working on fills the middle, at full size. Everything else waits at the sides as cards, getting smaller towards the edge, and never overlapping.

Focus

Full screen for anything

Give one item the whole window: a diagram to pan and zoom, a document to read, a table with room for every column.

Embedding

Design it once, use it everywhere

A standard like service auth is a schematic of its own, embedded as one card wherever it’s used, pinned to the draft it was built against.

Overview

The whole design at once

Every item full size on one canvas, laid out around what links to what, with one line for each pair of linked items.

Comments

Comments where you press

Each comment shows its thread, your agent’s reply and how it dealt with it, in its own words. Mark one as needing nothing more, or carry on the conversation.

Focuses

A goal for each change

Each draft says what it was for. Start a new change by saying what you’re after, and copy the prompt for your agent.

Windows

Every agent gets its own window

The top bar shows which agent is working in a window. Several can work at once without overwriting each other, and you can always take a window back.

Home

Domains, schematics, drafts

Schematics belong to the team or department that owns them, organised in folders that can follow your organisation.
